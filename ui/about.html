Nadir

A lightweight Tauri v2 system-webview browser shell. Nadir uses the OS webview (WebKitGTK on Linux, WebView2 on Windows, WKWebView on macOS) rather than bundling a browser engine.

Built-in pages: nadir://newtab, nadir://settings, nadir://history, nadir://bookmarks, nadir://downloads, nadir://error.
